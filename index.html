About Penguin Sled Rush

Penguin Sled Rush is a 3D sledding race. Steer Pip the penguin down an icy chute through Snowpeak Harbour: pine slopes, a frozen canal crossed by stone bridges and a lantern-lit village, all the way to the finish arch.

Hit blue boost pads for speed, launch off ramps for big air, hop over logs and fences, dodge snowmen and ice blocks, and grab golden fish for points. Finish fast to earn up to three stars and beat your best score.

3 quick tips

  1. Drag left or right anywhere to steer. Arrow keys or A/D work too.
  2. Swipe up, tap JUMP or press Space to hop over logs and fences.
  3. Longer air time on ramps scores more style points.

How to play

🐧 Race Pip down the Snowpeak Harbour run to the finish arch.

👆 Steer: drag left or right anywhere. Keyboard: ← → or A / D.

⤴️ Hop: swipe up, tap JUMP, or press Space. Hop over logs and fences.

💙 Boost pads give a burst of speed. Ramps launch you into the air: longer air = more style points.

⛄ Snowmen and ice blocks bonk you and slow you down.

🐟 Golden fish are worth 10 points each. Finish fast for a time bonus!
